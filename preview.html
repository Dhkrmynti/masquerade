The cast

Envoys carry a purse,
not your keys.

An envoy gets its own mask, a purse, a list of stocks and a curfew. The purse is the only money it can reach, so it is the hard limit.

  • Curfew. At the final bow it stops and waits for you.
  • Script. Only the stocks you named, nothing else.
  • Recall. Call it off stage any time; funds return to you.

The fine print

What a mask can and can't hide.

Privacy on a public chain has edges. Here they are, before the show.

A MASK HIDES
  • Your trades from your main wallet's own activity list
  • Your full position at a glance, split across masks
  • Which stocks your envoy is watching
A MASK DOES NOT HIDE
  • The funding transfer from your main wallet, which anyone can follow on-chain
  • Timing patterns, if you fund and trade in the same minute
  • Anything you post publicly about your trades
