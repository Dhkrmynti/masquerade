How it works

Three moves. No trail back to you.

Your main wallet funds a fresh address, the trade happens there, and the mask is retired. Anyone reading the chain sees a guest, not you.

Put on a mask

Masquerade derives a fresh address from your wallet for this one trade, so the trade has no shared history with your main wallet.

Trade behind it

Leave the ball

Envoys

Agents with a purse, not a key.

An envoy gets its own mask, a purse, a list of stocks and a curfew. The purse is the hard limit: it is the only money the envoy can reach.

  • Purse-capped. It can't spend what isn't in its mask.
  • Curfew. At expiry it stops and waits for you.
  • Whitelist. Only the stocks you named, nothing else.
  • Revocable. Call it home any time, funds return to you.

Honest limits

What a mask can and can't hide.

Privacy on a public chain has edges. Here they are, up front.

A MASK HIDES
  • The link between a trade and your main wallet's history
  • Your full position across all masks, from casual chain readers
  • Which stocks your envoy is watching
A MASK DOES NOT HIDE
  • The funding transaction itself, which is still on-chain
  • Timing patterns, if you fund and trade in the same minute
  • Anything you post publicly about your trades
